Who We Are

TECH SERVICES HUB INNOVATION is a Nigerian technology company that combines AI Automation, Full-Stack Web Development, and Solar Installation & Maintenance to deliver integrated, intelligent energy solutions.

Founded by Mmaduabuchi Ogu, an AI/ML and NLP certified engineer, we bridge the gap between cutting-edge technology and practical energy solutions for Nigerian homes and businesses.

Our mission is to make solar energy smarter, more efficient, and accessible to every Nigerian through the power of artificial intelligence and modern technology.

We are officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) and certified by SMEDAN, ensuring you receive professional, trusted service.
